Drawa

Write between the guide lines: the middle band is the x-height, the lower solid line is the baseline. Use natural, normal-size writing.

Characters to train

How it works: strokes of each sample are resampled to equal spacing and aligned, then every time a letter is written, a fresh random blend of your samples is made, plus smooth wobble, tilt and size drift. No two letters are identical and none is an exact copy. Samples are stored in this browser only.
